PRIVACY

Raster runs entirely on your computer. It has no accounts, analytics or
telemetry, and your art and files are never uploaded.

The installed version checks GitHub for new versions when it starts and
when you choose Help > Check for Updates. The request goes to github.com
and includes your IP address and the app version, as every web request
does. No personal information, files or usage data are sent.

To turn this off, open Help > About Raster and clear "Check for updates
when Raster starts".

Full privacy policy:
https://github.com/J-isaac007/raster/blob/main/docs/privacy.md
